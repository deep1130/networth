🏛️ New Jersey FIRE, Healthcare & State Tax Strategy GetCoveredNJ + NJ Tax Code
1. GetCoveredNJ + NJHPS Subsidies Up to 600% FPL

New Jersey operates its own exchange (GetCoveredNJ) and provides NJ Health Plan Savings (NJHPS)—a state-funded cash subsidy that stacks on top of federal Premium Tax Credits.

  • Extended Subsidy Cap: NJ provides state subsidies up to 600% FPL ($93,900/yr for single), far higher than the federal limit.
  • CSR Sweet Spot: If reportable MAGI is kept under 250% FPL (~$39,125), you qualify for Cost-Sharing Reductions (CSR)—Silver deductibles drop to $0–$500 with net premiums of $50–$165/mo.
2. The "Spend $100k, Report $35k" Strategy Portfolio Arbitrage

In early retirement, spending does NOT equal taxable income (MAGI). You can comfortably fund a $75k–$150k lifestyle while keeping ACA subsidies:

  • Zero-MAGI Draws: Cash reserves, taxable brokerage principal return, and Roth IRA contribution withdrawals generate $0 reportable MAGI.
  • Tax-Efficient Spending: Drawing $40k capital/Roth + $35k gains/dividends lets you spend $75,000–$100,000/yr while locking in full NJ Silver subsidies.
3. NJ Individual Health Mandate State Tax Penalty

Unlike federal law, New Jersey mandates coverage under the NJ Health Insurance Market Preservation Act.

  • Mandate Penalty: Uninsured NJ residents must pay a state penalty on Line 52 of Form NJ-1040 (the greater of $695/adult or 2.5% of gross income).
  • FIRE Takeaway: Going without insurance in NJ is financially penalized; maintaining a subsidized GetCoveredNJ plan is both safer and cheaper.
4. Other NJ Tax Codes for Early Retirees NJ-1040 Rules

Key New Jersey state tax idiosyncrasies to model in your wealth projections:

  • HSA Taxation: NJ is one of only two states (NJ & CA) that does NOT recognize HSAs. HSA contributions and yearly interest/dividends are taxable on your NJ-1040.
  • ANCHOR Benefit: Direct NJ cash rebate ($450/yr for renters, $1,000–$1,500/yr for homeowners) if gross income is under $150k/$250k.
  • Low Bracket During FIRE: At an optimized $35k MAGI, your NJ state tax rate is just 1.40% – 1.75%.
  • Age 62+ Pension Exclusion: Once you reach 62, NJ lets you exclude up to $100,000/yr in retirement income 100% tax-free if total income is ≤ $150k.
